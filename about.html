About

Marketer, occasional coder, full-time curious person.

Letter from me

Hi,

I'm a digital marketing specialist who somehow ended up enjoying both creative ideas and spreadsheets, which, honestly, I never saw coming.

I started out in media and communications, and over the years I've worked across social media, SEO, content, digital advertising, PR, UX copywriting, analytics, and growth marketing. Somewhere along the way, I also started getting curious about the technical side of marketing, such as building dashboards, playing around with code, tracking what happens after someone clicks a link, and occasionally asking Claude to help me fix something I probably broke myself.

What I enjoy most is figuring out why something works, why it doesn't, and what we can do about it. I like turning ideas into campaigns, campaigns into data, and data into better decisions.

I'm naturally curious and a little obsessed with learning new things, and I genuinely enjoy figuring things out as I go.

This portfolio is basically a collection of a few things I've worked on, built, tested, learned from, and occasionally overthought.

Thanks for stopping by. I hope you find something interesting here.

— Annie
