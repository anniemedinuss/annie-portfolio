Data2Bots — turning email campaigns and landing pages into a lead-generation engine.

Digital Marketing Generalist · Remote · Jun 2023 – Feb 2024

Email Marketing Digital Marketing SEO Google Analytics Content Marketing Social Media Management Copywriting Web Design
Client
Data2Bots
Industry
IT consulting & data solutions
Objective
Grow leads, engagement and brand visibility

Data2Bots is an IT consulting firm providing clients with innovative, effective data solutions — spanning Data2Bots Academy, TalentPipeline, Extended Team and Solutions, out of Almere, Netherlands. As their digital marketing generalist, I planned, executed and optimized their email marketing, landing pages, paid retargeting and content across every channel, working closely with the sales team to turn campaigns into qualified leads.

Increase lead generation — attract and convert potential leads more effectively.
Enhance client engagement — boost engagement across email, social and digital channels.
Brand awareness — strengthen Data2Bots' presence and market positioning.
Market positioning — partner with influencers and media to elevate visibility.
Strategy & execution
01

Email marketing campaigns

Strategically planned, executed and optimized email marketing campaigns on GetResponse — segmented lists, personalized content, and nurture flows built to move subscribers from open to click to lead.

−60% drop-off · +30% open rate · +40% click rate

02

Landing pages & lead capture

Built high-converting landing pages in GetResponse and integrated forms and pop-ups across the website to streamline lead generation — like the page below for Data2Bots Academy's Data Engineering career path.

+20% customer outreach

03

Analytics, A/B testing & retargeting

Monitored and analyzed every campaign with Google Analytics and GetResponse analytics, driving advanced A/B testing, novel marketing approaches and retargeting ad campaigns. Built out Google Tags and Facebook Pixels for advanced tracking and ran the PPC campaigns behind it.

04

Content, SEO & UX copywriting

Crafted engaging social media ad copy, email content and SEO copywriting using Grammarly and Quillbot to keep every line clean and on-brand, deployed internal links strategically to boost reader engagement, and wrote the UX and web copy for GetResponse-linked pages. Leveraged Canva to design visually appealing supporting content.

05

Social, influencer & brand campaigns

Worked closely with the sales team to generate leads, formed strategic partnerships with influencers and media personnel, and led ad campaigns and brand launches for Data2Bots Academy — growing YouTube subscribers and Telegram engagement alongside it. Task and sprint planning ran on Jira with Kanban Agile methodology throughout.

+20% YouTube subscribers

Results, impact & conclusion

Lead generation

A 20% increase in customer outreach, with stronger lead quality from targeted campaigns.

Client engagement

Email open and click rates climbed while drop-off fell — a healthier funnel end to end.

Brand awareness

Influencer partnerships and Academy campaign launches expanded Data2Bots' visibility.

Collaboration efficiency

Jira and Kanban sprint planning kept marketing and sales aligned on shared goals.

Across email, landing pages, paid retargeting and content, Data2Bots saw a 60% drop in email bounce-off, a 30% lift in open rates and 40% lift in click rates, alongside a 20% increase in customer outreach — turning scattered campaigns into one coordinated growth engine.
