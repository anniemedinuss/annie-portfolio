Rentease — taking a rent-financing product from zero to a national launch.

Growth Marketing Manager, Product Launch · Remote · Sep 2023 – Jan 2024

Digital Marketing SEO Google Analytics Google Ads Public Relations Copywriting Media Buying Content Marketing Social Media Management Animation Creation
Client
Rentease
Industry
Rent financing / proptech
Objective
Launch the product, build the brand, generate leads

Rentease helps people pay rent, renew a lease, or finance a new home when they're short on funds. I came on to take their new rent-financing product from an internal idea to a fully-marketed public launch — owning strategy and execution across PR, paid media, content, and organic growth, starting from zero press history and zero social following.

Product launch — introduce Rentease's financing product to the market.
Brand awareness — establish Rentease as a name people recognize.
Audience engagement — grow activity across social, email and SMS.
Lead generation — build campaigns that convert, not just reach.
Online presence — grow social following and website traffic from nothing.
Strategy & execution
01

Product launch & PR

Wrote and issued the press release announcing Rentease's launch, positioning its value proposition for a proptech audience. It earned coverage in The Punch, with additional pickup on Pulse.ng under Rentease's own hashtag. Paired the launch with strategic media buying on RadioNow 95.3FM, booking Rentease onto Tech Now to talk "Property and Technology" in front of a broader, non-digital audience.

Press coverage on Pulse.ng.

02

Content & copywriting

Wrote every word the product launched with — email, SMS and social copy, Google Ads copy, and scripts for animations and voice-over artists — keeping messaging consistent across every platform and touchpoint, from a cold DM to a radio spot.

03

Social media growth

Grew Rentease's social presence from nothing through consistent content and targeted ad campaigns:

Instagram 0 → 2,000+ · Twitter 0 → 500+ · LinkedIn 0 → 300+
all within 3 months.

The reach kept compounding past launch: a later 90-day reporting window logged 5.2M+ impressions and 1.1M+ accounts reached beyond Rentease's own following, with one reel alone pulling 503K views.

Instagram reach over a later 90-day window, and one X post's engagement on its own.

04

Paid media & retargeting

Designed and ran media campaigns focused on lead generation and Return on Ad Spend, then layered in Google Ads retargeting through Meta Pixel and Google Tag Manager to re-engage visitors who had interacted with an ad but hadn't yet converted.

05

SEO & website traffic

Took website traffic from 100+ to 23,000+ visitors in 3 months by pairing paid distribution with SEO content built around Google Keyword Planner research — 11K new users and 38K views in the most recent 90-day window shown below.

06

Systems, testing & in-house creative

The unglamorous work that kept everything above running.

Platform management

Set up and managed every media platform from scratch, building a presence consistent enough to carry the brand past launch day.

A/B testing

Ran ongoing tests on ad copy, visuals and targeting, using the results to keep refining spend efficiency and ROI.

Graphics & animation

Produced the campaign's graphics and motion assets in Canva, Adobe Creative Suite, InShot, CapCut and Jitter.

Results, impact & conclusion

Brand awareness

Took the launch from an internal product to national press coverage, securing a real foothold in mainstream media.

Social media growth

Built a genuine social presence from zero, growing every channel at once — and kept compounding to 5.2M+ impressions and 1.1M+ accounts reached in a later 90-day stretch.

Lead generation

Paid campaigns converted at a healthy rate, delivering a strong Return on Ad Spend rather than vanity reach.

Website traffic

230x growth in visitors in 3 months, turning the website into a real, ongoing acquisition channel.

SEO success

Search-driven content pushed rankings up and kept traffic compounding well past the initial launch push.

A tightly coordinated launch — PR, paid media, content and organic all pulling in the same direction — took Rentease from an unlaunched product to a recognized name with a fast-growing digital footprint: 23,000+ monthly visitors, and 2,000+ Instagram, 500+ Twitter and 300+ LinkedIn followers, all built from zero in just three months.
