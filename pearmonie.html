PearMonie — writing the copy and UX content behind an all-in-one business platform.

Copywriter / UX Content Developer

UX Content Development Copywriting Google Analytics Content Marketing Social Media Copywriting Google Ads Copywriting Video Script Writing Email Sequence Development
Client
PearMonie
Industry
E-commerce & business management
Objective
Sharpen the brand's voice, UX and conversion

PearMonie is a dynamic e-commerce and business-management platform built to help small businesses and freelancers run their operations from one place. Brought on as their dedicated copywriter and UX content developer, I shaped their communication strategy end to end — writing for social media, Google Ads, video scripts, email sequences, SMS and push notifications, and the in-app copy itself.

Enhance brand visibility — through strategic social media and Google Ads copy.
Boost user engagement — with video scripts, email sequences and SMS/push notifications.
Improve user experience — clear, friendly UX content across the website and app.
Drive conversions — optimized copy guiding users through the funnel to sign-up.
Strategy & execution
01

UX content development

Rewrote the website and app copy for PearMonie's new feature rollout — onboarding flows, feature descriptions and microcopy — to make the product feel simpler to pick up and easier to trust. The clip below shows the site before and after: same product, a clearer story.

02

Influencer & ad campaign copywriting

Developed the video script and ad copy for KieKie's influencer campaign, reviewing every cut of the script and working closely with the content team to keep the messaging on-brand while it still felt native to her audience.

KieKie's ad campaign.

03

Content marketing & SEO

Crafted blog content tailored to PearMonie's target audience, incorporating essential keywords throughout to support their organic search efforts and give the ad and social campaigns a home to convert visitors into readers.

04

Paid media copywriting

Wrote the advert copy for PearMonie's placements through Braken Media, keeping every line aligned to brand objectives, then worked closely with the design team so the copy and the visuals landed as one cohesive message.

05

Broadcast & TV copywriting

Developed the copy for a PearMonie advertisement that aired on Zee World, one of Nigeria's most-watched TV channels — copy that had to work read aloud, at broadcast pace, for an audience outside PearMonie's usual digital reach. It was accepted by a recognized Nigerian advertising body, a mark of how tightly it had to be written.

PearMonie × Zee World TV spot.

06

Lifecycle & retention copywriting

Wrote the SMS and push notification copy that kept users coming back — short, timely, action-oriented messages covering everything from bill reminders to product tips, tuned to land at the moment they'd actually be useful.

Engagement rate +100%

Results, impact & conclusion

Brand visibility

Social and Google Ads copy gave PearMonie a sharper, more consistent voice across every channel.

User engagement

Video, email and SMS copy turned passive users into people who actually opened, watched and clicked.

User experience

Rewritten UX content made the website and app easier to navigate, from onboarding to feature usage.

Conversions

Ad and UX copy worked together to guide more visitors through the funnel to sign-up.

Retention

Timely SMS and push copy kept users returning, doubling engagement on those channels.

Through copywriting and UX content spanning web, social, paid and lifecycle channels, PearMonie saw a 30% lift in social engagement, a 25% jump in ad click-through, and a 100% increase in SMS and push engagement — with copy strong enough to earn TV placement on Zee World along the way.
